## The Mathematics of Time and Age

Calculating the exact difference between two dates is more complex than standard subtraction because the Gregorian calendar is highly irregular. 

1. **Varying Months:** Months have 28, 29, 30, or 31 days.
2. **Leap Years:** Every four years, an extra day is added to February to keep the calendar synchronized with the astronomical year.
3. **Exceptions to Leap Years:** Years divisible by 100 are NOT leap years, unless they are also divisible by 400 (which is why the year 2000 was a leap year, but 1900 was not).

### How to Calculate Age Manually

To calculate age manually, you align the birth date and the current date (or target date) and subtract the days, then the months, and finally the years.

**Example Calculation:**
- **Birth Date:** May 15, 1990
- **Target Date:** August 10, 2025

*Step 1: Subtract the Days*
10 (Target) - 15 (Birth). Since you cannot have negative days, you must "borrow" a month from August. August becomes July, and you add 31 days (the length of July) to the 10.
$41 - 15 = 26 \text{ days}$.

*Step 2: Subtract the Months*
7 (July) - 5 (May) = 2 months.

*Step 3: Subtract the Years*
2025 - 1990 = 35 years.

The exact age is **35 years, 2 months, and 26 days**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do some calculators give me a different number of days?**
If a calculator ignores the strict rules of leap years or assumes every month has exactly 30.44 days, the final day count will be slightly inaccurate. Our calculator uses precise chronological mathematics, checking every single month and leap year between the two dates.

**How do you convert human years to dog years?**
The old rule of "1 human year equals 7 dog years" is a myth. The American Veterinary Medical Association states that the first year of a medium-sized dog's life is equivalent to 15 human years. The second year equals 9 human years. After that, every human year equals about 5 dog years.

**What is a chronological age?**
Your chronological age is the exact amount of time that has passed from your birth to a given date, measured strictly by the calendar. It is distinct from your "biological age," which is a medical assessment of how fast your cells are deteriorating based on lifestyle and genetics.
