## About the Area Calculator

Our **Area Calculator** computes the two-dimensional surface area for all common geometric shapes. Area calculations are essential in construction (material estimation), farming (field coverage), interior design (flooring, wallpaper), and everyday problem solving.

---

## Area Formulas for Common Shapes

### Rectangle
$$ A = l \times w $$

### Circle
$$ A = \pi r^2 $$

### Triangle
$$ A = \frac{1}{2} \times b \times h $$

### Trapezoid
$$ A = \frac{(a + b)}{2} \times h $$
*(where $a$ and $b$ are the parallel sides, $h$ is the height)*

### Ellipse
$$ A = \pi \times a \times b $$
*(where $a$ and $b$ are the semi-major and semi-minor axes)*

### Regular Polygon (n sides, side length s)
$$ A = \frac{ns^2}{4} \cot\left(\frac{\pi}{n}\right) $$

---

**Example (Circle):** A circular garden with a 7-meter radius:
$$ A = \pi \times 7^2 = \pi \times 49 \approx 153.94 \text{ m}^2 $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between area and perimeter?**
Area measures the 2D surface *inside* a shape (measured in square units). Perimeter measures the total *boundary length* around a shape (measured in linear units). A 10m × 10m square has an area of 100 m² and a perimeter of 40 m.

**How do I find the area of an irregular shape?**
Break it into smaller recognizable shapes (rectangles, triangles, semicircles). Calculate each area separately, then add (or subtract for holes) them together.
