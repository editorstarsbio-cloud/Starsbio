## About the Bill Split Calculator

Splitting a restaurant bill fairly can be surprisingly complicated — especially when different people ordered different amounts, or when tip and tax need to be divided proportionally. Our **Bill Split Calculator** handles every scenario instantly.

---

## Simple Equal Split

$$ \text{Per Person} = \frac{\text{Total Bill} + \text{Tip}}{N} $$

**Example:** A $120 bill (including tax) split by 4 people with a 20% tip:
- Tip = $120 × 0.20 = $24
- Total with tip = $144
- Per person = $144 ÷ 4 = **$36**

---

## Proportional Split (Based on What Each Person Ordered)

When people ordered very different amounts, a proportional split is fairer:

$$ \text{Person A's Share} = \frac{\text{Person A's food total}}{\text{Subtotal}} \times \text{Grand Total (with tip & tax)} $$

**Example:** Total $100 bill (before tip). Three people ordered $30, $40, $30.
- Person B (who ordered $40): $\frac{40}{100} \times 120 = \$48$ (with 20% tip)
- Person A and C: $36 each

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should tax be added before calculating tip?**
Most etiquette guides say you should tip on the pre-tax amount, since you are rewarding the service, not the government. However, in practice many people tip on the post-tax total for simplicity since the difference is small.

**What if someone doesn't drink alcohol?**
Alcohol can account for 30–40% of a restaurant bill. Non-drinkers have a legitimate reason to request a proportional rather than equal split. The most fair approach is to total each person's actual orders (including their share of shared appetizers), then split tax and tip proportionally.
