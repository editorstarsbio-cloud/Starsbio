## About the Body Fat Calculator

Our **Body Fat Percentage Calculator** estimates the proportion of your total body weight that consists of fat mass. Unlike BMI, which only accounts for height and weight, body fat percentage directly measures your fat-to-lean-mass ratio, making it a far more accurate indicator of physical fitness.

> **Medical Disclaimer:** This tool provides an estimate only. Results are not a medical diagnosis. Consult a healthcare professional for clinical body composition assessment.

## How the US Navy Formula Works

The most accessible body fat estimation method (no expensive equipment required) is the **US Navy Circumference Method**:

**For Men:**
$$ \%\text{BF} = 86.01 \times \log_{10}(\text{waist} - \text{neck}) - 70.041 \times \log_{10}(\text{height}) + 36.76 $$

**For Women:**
$$ \%\text{BF} = 163.205 \times \log_{10}(\text{waist} + \text{hip} - \text{neck}) - 97.684 \times \log_{10}(\text{height}) - 78.387 $$

*(All measurements in centimeters or inches consistently.)*

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the most accurate way to measure body fat?**
From most to least accurate: DEXA scan > Hydrostatic weighing > Air displacement plethysmography (Bod Pod) > Bioelectrical Impedance (gym scales) > Circumference formulas > BMI. The US Navy method is surprisingly accurate (within 3–4%) for the general population at zero cost.

**Can I have a normal BMI but high body fat?**
Yes. This is called "normal weight obesity" or being "skinny fat." A person with very little muscle mass and high fat mass can have a BMI within the normal range while carrying an unhealthy amount of body fat, carrying elevated health risks.
