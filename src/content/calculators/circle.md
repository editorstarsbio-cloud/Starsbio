## About the Circle Calculator

A **circle** is defined as the set of all points in a plane equidistant from a central point. Our **Circle Calculator** instantly computes any circle property — circumference, area, diameter, radius — from just one known value.

---

## Key Circle Formulas

From the **radius** ($r$), all other measurements follow:

| Property | Formula |
|:---|:---|
| Diameter | $d = 2r$ |
| Circumference | $C = 2\pi r = \pi d$ |
| Area | $A = \pi r^2$ |

Where $\pi \approx 3.14159265...$

**Example:** A circular swimming pool with a diameter of 8 meters:
- Radius: $r = 8/2 = 4$ m
- Circumference: $C = 2\pi(4) = 8\pi \approx 25.13$ m
- Area: $A = \pi(4)^2 = 16\pi \approx 50.27$ m²

## Sector and Arc Calculations

For a circle **sector** (a "pie slice") with central angle $\theta$ (in degrees):

$$ \text{Arc Length} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r $$
$$ \text{Sector Area} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2 $$

**Example:** A sector with $r = 5$ m and $\theta = 90°$:
- Arc Length = $\frac{90}{360} \times 2\pi(5) = \frac{1}{4} \times 31.42 = 7.85$ m
- Sector Area = $\frac{90}{360} \times \pi(25) = \frac{1}{4} \times 78.54 = 19.63$ m²

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is $\pi$ irrational?**
$\pi$ is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter. It was proven irrational in 1761 by Johann Lambert — meaning it cannot be expressed as a simple fraction and its decimal expansion never repeats or terminates. Over a trillion digits of $\pi$ have been calculated.

**What is the circumference of the Earth?**
The Earth is not a perfect sphere, but at the equator its circumference is approximately 40,075 km (24,901 miles). This was first calculated by the ancient Greek mathematician Eratosthenes around 240 BCE with surprising accuracy.
