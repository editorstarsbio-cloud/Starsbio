## About the Discount Calculator

Whether you are shopping online, comparing sale prices, or running a promotional campaign, our **Discount Calculator** instantly tells you the sale price and how much money you save.

## The Formulas

$$ \text{Savings} = \text{Original Price} \times \left(\frac{\text{Discount \%}}{100}\right) $$
$$ \text{Sale Price} = \text{Original Price} - \text{Savings} $$

**Example:** A $250 jacket at 30% off:
$$ \text{Savings} = 250 \times 0.30 = \$75 $$
$$ \text{Sale Price} = 250 - 75 = \$175 $$

### Finding the Discount Percentage

If you know the original and sale price but want to know the discount percentage:

$$ \text{Discount \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Original Price} - \text{Sale Price}}{\text{Original Price}}\right) \times 100 $$

## Stacked Discounts

Retailers sometimes advertise "an extra 20% off sale items." Stacked discounts do NOT simply add together. A 30% off item that then gets an additional 20% off gives:

$$ \text{Final Price} = \text{Original} \times (1 - 0.30) \times (1 - 0.20) $$
$$ = 100 \times 0.70 \times 0.80 = \$56 $$

The combined effective discount is **44%**, not 50%.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I reverse-calculate the original price from a sale price?**
If a sale price is $80 after a 20% discount:
$$ \text{Original} = \frac{\text{Sale Price}}{1 - \text{Discount}} = \frac{80}{0.80} = \$100 $$

**Why does 50% off + 50% off not equal free?**
Because the second 50% is applied to the already-reduced price. A $100 item at 50% off = $50. A further 50% off $50 = $25. The total effective discount is 75%, not 100%.
