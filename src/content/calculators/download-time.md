## About the Download Time Calculator

Our **Download Time Calculator** tells you exactly how long it will take to download or upload a file based on your internet connection speed. Understanding transfer speeds and times is essential for planning large file downloads, video streaming setups, and network provisioning.

---

## Key Formula

$$ \text{Time (seconds)} = \frac{\text{File Size (bits)}}{\text{Connection Speed (bps)}} $$

Since file sizes are typically shown in Megabytes (MB) and speeds in Megabits per second (Mbps), the most important conversion to remember is:

**1 Byte = 8 bits**

So 1 MB = 8 Mb (Megabits). A 100 MB file contains 800 Megabits of data.

**Example:** Downloading a 4 GB (4,000 MB = 32,000 Mb) game on a 100 Mbps connection:
$$ \text{Time} = \frac{32,000 \text{ Mb}}{100 \text{ Mbps}} = 320 \text{ seconds} \approx 5 \text{ minutes 20 seconds} $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is my actual download slower than my plan speed?**
Internet service providers advertise maximum theoretical speeds. Real-world speeds are lower due to network congestion, Wi-Fi overhead (Wi-Fi is typically 50–70% as fast as a wired ethernet connection), server-side limitations, and distance from your router.

**What is the difference between Mbps and MBps?**
Mbps = Megabits per second (network speed). MBps = Megabytes per second (file size rate). Your 100 Mbps plan downloads at 12.5 MBps because $100 \div 8 = 12.5$.
