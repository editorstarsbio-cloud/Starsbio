## How Does Fraction Math Work?

A fraction consists of two parts: a **numerator** (the top number, representing how many parts you have) and a **denominator** (the bottom number, representing how many parts make up a whole).

### Adding and Subtracting Fractions

To add or subtract fractions, they must have a **Common Denominator**. 

$$ \frac{a}{c} + \frac{b}{c} = \frac{a + b}{c} $$

**Example:**
$$ \frac{1}{4} + \frac{2}{4} = \frac{3}{4} $$

If the denominators are different, you must multiply them to find a common ground before adding:
$$ \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} $$
Multiply the top and bottom of the first fraction by 3, and the second by 2:
$$ \frac{3}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{5}{6} $$

### Multiplying Fractions

Multiplying fractions is much simpler. You do not need a common denominator. You simply multiply the numerators together, and multiply the denominators together.

$$ \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \times c}{b \times d} $$

**Example:**
$$ \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{8}{15} $$

### Dividing Fractions

To divide fractions, you use the "Keep-Change-Flip" method. Keep the first fraction, change the division sign to multiplication, and flip the second fraction (this is called the reciprocal).

$$ \frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c} $$

**Example:**
$$ \frac{1}{2} \div \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3} $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is a mixed number?**
A mixed number is a whole number combined with a fraction, such as $1 \frac{1}{2}$. To calculate with mixed numbers, you usually convert them into improper fractions first (where the numerator is larger than the denominator, such as $\frac{3}{2}$).

**How do I simplify or reduce a fraction?**
To simplify a fraction, you must find the Greatest Common Factor (GCF) of both the numerator and the denominator, and divide them both by that number. For example, in the fraction $\frac{4}{8}$, both numbers are divisible by 4. Dividing them by 4 gives you the simplified fraction of $\frac{1}{2}$. Our calculator does this automatically.
