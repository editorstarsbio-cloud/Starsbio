## About the Fuel Economy Calculator

Our **Fuel Economy Calculator** helps you figure out exactly how far your car can travel on a gallon (or liter) of fuel and how much your trips actually cost. Whether you are comparing vehicles before a purchase, tracking monthly fuel expenses, or planning a road trip budget, this tool provides instant clarity.

---

## Key Formulas

**Miles Per Gallon (MPG):**
$$ \text{MPG} = \frac{\text{Distance (miles)}}{\text{Fuel Used (gallons)}} $$

**Liters per 100 km (L/100km):**
$$ \text{L/100km} = \frac{\text{Fuel Used (liters)}}{\text{Distance (km)}} \times 100 $$

**Trip Fuel Cost:**
$$ \text{Cost} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{MPG}} \times \text{Price per Gallon} $$

**Example:** Drive 350 miles using 12 gallons of gas at $3.50/gallon:
$$ \text{MPG} = \frac{350}{12} = 29.17 $$
$$ \text{Cost} = \frac{350}{29.17} \times 3.50 = \$42.00 $$

## City vs. Highway MPG

The EPA tests every new vehicle under standardized city and highway driving cycles. Actual real-world fuel economy typically runs **15–20% lower** than EPA estimates because of:
- Air conditioning use
- Aggressive acceleration and braking
- Cold engine warm-up cycles
- Roof racks and cargo weight
- Tire pressure (under-inflated tires reduce MPG by 0.2% per PSI)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I convert MPG to L/100km?**
$$ \text{L/100km} = \frac{235.21}{\text{MPG}} $$
A car getting 30 MPG gets $235.21 / 30 = 7.84$ L/100km.

**Does cruise control save fuel?**
Yes. On flat highways, cruise control reduces unnecessary speed fluctuations and can improve fuel economy by 7–14%.

**How much does driving 10 mph faster cost in fuel?**
Fuel economy drops roughly 7–14% for every 10 mph increase above 50 mph. Slowing from 75 to 65 mph on a long highway trip can meaningfully cut your fuel bill.
