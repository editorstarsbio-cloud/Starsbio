## About the Pace Calculator

Our **Pace Calculator** is the go-to tool for runners, cyclists, and triathletes. It calculates your pace (time per mile or kilometer), finish time for a given distance, or the distance you can cover in a set time — all from just two known values.

---

## The Formula

$$ \text{Pace} = \frac{\text{Time}}{\text{Distance}} $$
$$ \text{Finish Time} = \text{Pace} \times \text{Distance} $$
$$ \text{Distance} = \frac{\text{Time}}{\text{Pace}} $$

**Example:** You run a 10K (10 km) in 52 minutes 30 seconds (52.5 minutes):
$$ \text{Pace} = \frac{52.5}{10} = 5.25 \text{ min/km} = 5 \text{ min } 15 \text{ sec per km} $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I convert pace between km and miles?**
To convert min/km to min/mile, multiply by 1.60934.
To convert min/mile to min/km, divide by 1.60934.
A 5:00 min/km pace equals 8:03 min/mile.

**What pace do I need to run a sub-4-hour marathon?**
A marathon is 26.2188 miles. For exactly 4 hours (240 minutes):
$$ \text{Pace} = \frac{240}{26.2188} \approx 9:09 \text{ per mile} = 5:41 \text{ per km} $$
