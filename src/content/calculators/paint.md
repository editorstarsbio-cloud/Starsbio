## About the Paint Calculator

Our **Paint Calculator** helps you estimate exactly how many gallons (or liters) of paint you need for any room or surface. Buying too much wastes money; buying too little means an extra trip to the hardware store and the risk of a different dye lot.

---

## How to Calculate Paint Needed

**Step 1: Calculate total wall area**
$$ \text{Wall Area} = \text{Perimeter of Room} \times \text{Ceiling Height} $$

For a room 12 ft × 10 ft with 9 ft ceilings:
- Perimeter = $2 \times (12 + 10) = 44$ ft
- Wall Area = $44 \times 9 = 396$ sq ft

**Step 2: Subtract doors and windows**
- Standard door: ~20 sq ft each
- Standard window: ~15 sq ft each

If the room has 1 door and 2 windows: $396 - 20 - 30 = 346$ sq ft to paint.

**Step 3: Divide by coverage rate**
Most standard paints cover **350–400 sq ft per gallon** for the first coat.
$$ \text{Gallons Needed} = \frac{346}{350} \approx 1 \text{ gallon per coat} $$

For 2 coats: 2 gallons. Add 10% buffer: **2.2 gallons → round up to 3 quarts or 1 gallon + 1 quart**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need two coats?**
Almost always. Most professionals recommend a minimum of two coats for even, durable coverage. If you are making a dramatic color change (e.g., dark to light), you may need a primer coat plus two finish coats.

**Should I buy extra paint?**
Yes. Keep 1 quart of leftover paint for touch-ups. Paint from different production batches can vary in color.
