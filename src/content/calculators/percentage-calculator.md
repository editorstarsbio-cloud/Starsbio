## How to Calculate Percentages (Formulas)

Understanding the underlying math behind percentage calculations is important. A percentage is simply a ratio expressed as a fraction of 100. The word "percent" originates from the Latin *per centum*, which literally translates to "by a hundred".

Here are the three most common percentage formulas:

### 1. How to find the percentage of a number
If you want to find $Y\%$ of $X$, you can use the following formula:

$$ \text{Result} = X \times \left(\frac{Y}{100}\right) $$

**Example:** What is 15% of 200?
$$ 200 \times \left(\frac{15}{100}\right) = 200 \times 0.15 = 30 $$

### 2. How to find what percentage $X$ is of $Y$
If you need to know what percentage a certain number is compared to another, use this formula:

$$ \text{Percentage} = \left(\frac{X}{Y}\right) \times 100 $$

**Example:** 40 is what percent of 160?
$$ \left(\frac{40}{160}\right) \times 100 = 0.25 \times 100 = 25\% $$

### 3. How to find the total value if $X$ is $Y\%$
If you know the part and the percentage and want to find the whole, you rearrange the formula:

$$ \text{Total} = \frac{X}{\left(\frac{Y}{100}\right)} $$

**Example:** 50 is 20% of what number?
$$ \frac{50}{0.20} = 250 $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does percentage mean?**
A percentage is a number or ratio expressed as a fraction of 100. It is often denoted using the percent sign, "%". For example, 45% is equivalent to the fraction 45/100 or the decimal 0.45.

**How do I calculate a percentage increase or decrease?**
To calculate the percentage change between two numbers, subtract the old value from the new value, divide the result by the old value, and then multiply by 100.
$$ \text{Change} = \frac{\text{New} - \text{Old}}{\text{Old}} \times 100 $$
We also offer a dedicated Percentage Change Calculator for this specific purpose!

**Can a percentage be over 100?**
Yes. While you cannot have more than 100% of a single physical object (you cannot eat 110% of a pizza), percentages over 100 are frequently used in mathematics to represent growth. If your business revenue doubles, it has increased by 100%, bringing your total to 200% of what it was originally.
