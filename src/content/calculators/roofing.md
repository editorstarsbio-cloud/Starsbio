## About the Roofing Calculator

Replacing a roof is one of the most expensive maintenance tasks for a homeowner. Ordering the correct amount of shingles is vital—too few delays the project and risks mismatched batches, while too many wastes thousands of dollars. Our **Roofing Calculator** accurately estimates the materials you need.

## What is a "Square" in Roofing?

Roofers do not measure materials in individual square feet. They measure in **"Squares."**

**1 Roofing Square = 100 Square Feet.**

When you buy shingles, they are usually sold in bundles. It typically takes exactly **3 bundles** of standard 3-tab architectural shingles to cover 1 Square.

---

## Calculating Roof Area

If your roof is completely flat, the calculation is simple ($Length \times Width$). But most residential roofs are pitched (sloped). Because a sloped roof forms a triangle, the actual surface area of the roof is larger than the square footage of the house underneath it.

$$ \text{Roof Area} = \text{House Square Footage} \times \text{Pitch Multiplier} $$

### Finding the Pitch Multiplier
Roof pitch is expressed as a ratio of rise over run (e.g., 6/12 means the roof rises 6 inches for every 12 inches of horizontal run).
- **4/12 Pitch:** Multiplier is ~1.05
- **6/12 Pitch:** Multiplier is ~1.12
- **9/12 Pitch:** Multiplier is ~1.25
- **12/12 Pitch (very steep):** Multiplier is ~1.41

**Example:**
Your house footprint is 2,000 square feet, and you have a 6/12 roof pitch.
1. Total Roof Area = $2,000 \times 1.12 = 2,240 \text{ sq ft}$.
2. Convert to Squares = $2,240 / 100 = \mathbf{22.4 \text{ Squares}}$.
3. Number of Bundles = $22.4 \times 3 = \mathbf{67.2 \text{ Bundles}}$.

---

## Waste Factor

You can never buy the exact mathematical amount of roofing material. You must account for cutting shingles to fit around valleys, chimneys, and edges, as well as starter strips and ridge caps. 

- **Simple Gable Roof (Two flat sides):** Add 10% for waste.
- **Complex Hip Roof (Many valleys and dormers):** Add 15% to 20% for waste.

In the example above, a 15% waste factor means ordering $67.2 \times 1.15 = \mathbf{78 \text{ Bundles}}$.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to tear off the old roof?**
Building codes in many areas allow up to two layers of shingles on a roof. However, roofing over existing shingles traps heat, adds massive weight to the house structure, and prevents the roofer from inspecting the wooden decking underneath for rot. A full tear-off is almost always recommended for longevity.

**How many nails do I need?**
Standard installation requires 4 nails per shingle, which equates to roughly 320 nails per Square. In high-wind areas, code requires 6 nails per shingle (480 nails per Square). A standard box of coil roofing nails contains 7,200 nails, which covers about 20 Squares.
