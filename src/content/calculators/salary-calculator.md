Our **Salary Calculator** is a highly practical tool for converting your income across different time periods. ## How Are Salary Conversions Calculated?

The math behind salary conversion is based on standard, full-time working hours in a standard calendar year. 

### The Standard Assumptions
Before doing the math, it is important to understand the universally accepted baselines for a standard full-time job:
- **1 Year** = 52 weeks
- **1 Work Week** = 40 hours
- **1 Work Day** = 8 hours
- **Total Work Hours in a Year** = 2,080 hours ($52 \text{ weeks} \times 40 \text{ hours}$)

### Converting Annual Salary to Hourly Wage

To find out how much you make per hour from an annual salary, divide the total salary by 2,080.

$$ \text{Hourly Wage} = \frac{\text{Annual Salary}}{2080} $$

**Example Calculation:**
If you are offered a salary of $60,000 per year:
$$ \text{Hourly Wage} = \frac{60000}{2080} \approx \$28.85 \text{ per hour} $$

### Converting Hourly Wage to Annual Salary

If you work hourly and want to estimate your gross yearly income, multiply your hourly rate by 2,080.

$$ \text{Annual Salary} = \text{Hourly Wage} \times 2080 $$

**Example Calculation:**
If you make $15 an hour:
$$ \text{Annual Salary} = 15 \times 2080 = \$31,200 \text{ per year} $$

## Understanding Different Pay Periods

When budgeting your money, knowing the exact number of paychecks you will receive in a year is critical.

1. **Monthly:** Paid once a month (12 paychecks per year).
   - *Calculation:* Annual Salary ÷ 12
2. **Semi-Monthly:** Paid twice a month, usually on the 1st and 15th (24 paychecks per year).
   - *Calculation:* Annual Salary ÷ 24
3. **Bi-Weekly:** Paid every two weeks, usually every other Friday (26 paychecks per year).
   - *Calculation:* Annual Salary ÷ 26
4. **Weekly:** Paid once a week (52 paychecks per year).
   - *Calculation:* Annual Salary ÷ 52

*Note: In a bi-weekly system, there are two months every year where you will receive three paychecks instead of two. Budgeting experts refer to these as "magic months."*

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this calculator account for taxes?**
No. This calculator computes your **Gross Pay** (the total amount of money you earn before any taxes, insurance premiums, or retirement contributions are deducted). Your **Net Pay** (take-home pay) will be significantly lower and depends entirely on your local tax brackets and personal deductions.

**What if I work more or less than 40 hours a week?**
The standard conversion assumes a 40-hour workweek. If you consistently work 35 hours a week, your total annual hours drop to 1,820 ($35 \times 52$). You must adjust the math accordingly to get an accurate hourly rate.

**How is unpaid time off factored in?**
If you do not receive paid vacation days and take two weeks off per year, you only work 50 weeks instead of 52. Your total annual hours would be 2,000 ($40 \times 50$). An hourly worker making $20/hr would then earn $40,000 a year instead of $41,600.
