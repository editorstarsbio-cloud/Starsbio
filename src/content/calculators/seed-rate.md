## About the Seed Rate Calculator

In agriculture, the **Seed Rate** is the amount of seed required to plant a specific area of land to achieve a desired plant population. Whether you are sowing wheat, corn, or soybeans, calculating the precise seed rate is the first step to maximizing crop yield and minimizing wasted money on excess seed.

---

## The Formula

The formula for calculating the required seed rate (in pounds or kilograms per acre/hectare) takes into account the physical characteristics of the seed and the expected survival rate in the field.

$$ \text{Seed Rate} = \frac{\text{Target Plant Population} \times \text{Seed Weight}}{\text{Germination \%} \times \text{Purity \%} \times \text{Establishment \%}} $$

Where:
- **Target Population:** The ideal number of mature plants you want per acre.
- **Seed Weight:** Usually expressed as "Thousand Seed Weight" (TSW) in grams, or seeds per pound.
- **Germination %:** The percentage of seeds that are viable (found on the seed tag).
- **Purity %:** The percentage of the bag that is actually seed, not chaff or weed seeds (found on the seed tag).
- **Establishment (Emergence) %:** An estimate of how many germinated seeds will survive field conditions (birds, frost, insects).

**Example:**
You want 30,000 corn plants per acre. The seed tag says 95% germination and 99% purity. You expect 90% field emergence. The bag has 1,500 seeds per pound.
1. Total Viability = $0.95 \times 0.99 \times 0.90 = 0.846$ (Only 84.6% of seeds will become mature plants).
2. Seeds Needed = $30,000 / 0.846 = 35,461 \text{ seeds per acre}$.
3. Pounds Needed = $35,461 / 1,500 = \mathbf{23.6 \text{ lbs per acre}}$.

---

## Why Seed Rate is Critical

1. **Too High (Over-seeding):** You waste money buying expensive seed. More importantly, the plants will overcrowd, compete for limited soil moisture and nitrogen, and produce smaller, lower-quality yields. Lodging (plants falling over) becomes a major risk.
2. **Too Low (Under-seeding):** You leave open space in the canopy. Weeds will aggressively invade the empty soil, and your overall yield per acre will drop significantly because you don't have enough plants to capture the available sunlight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is TSW (Thousand Seed Weight)?**
TSW is the weight, usually in grams, of exactly 1,000 seeds. Because seed size varies wildly even within the same crop depending on the growing season, a farmer cannot just plant "100 pounds per acre" every year. 100 pounds of small seeds will result in vastly more plants than 100 pounds of large seeds. TSW allows you to plant by *population* rather than just weight.
