## About the Square Footage Calculator

Knowing the exact square footage of a room, property, or lot is essential for purchasing flooring, estimating paint, calculating property taxes, pricing a rental, or comparing home sizes. Our **Square Footage Calculator** handles simple rectangles as well as complex irregular shapes.

---

## Formulas for Common Shapes

### Rectangle or Square
$$ \text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} $$

### Triangle
$$ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Base} \times \text{Height} $$

### Circle
$$ \text{Area} = \pi \times r^2 $$
*(where $r$ is the radius — half the diameter)*

### L-Shaped Room
Break it into two rectangles, calculate each area separately, then add them together.

**Example:** A living room 15 ft × 12 ft:
$$ \text{Area} = 15 \times 12 = 180 \text{ sq ft} $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I measure square footage for an irregular room?**
Break the room into smaller rectangles or triangles. Measure and calculate each shape's area individually, then sum all the areas. Sketch the room on paper first to plan your measurements.

**Does square footage include walls?**
No. Square footage measures floor area only (horizontal surface). Wall area is calculated separately when estimating paint. To estimate paint, multiply wall height by wall perimeter, then subtract doors and windows.

**What is the difference between square feet and linear feet?**
Square feet measure a two-dimensional area (surface). Linear feet measure a single dimension (length). When buying trim, baseboards, or fencing, you need linear feet. When buying flooring or carpet, you need square feet.
