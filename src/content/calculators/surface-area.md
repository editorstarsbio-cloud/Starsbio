## About the Surface Area Calculator

**Surface area** is the total area of all outer surfaces of a three-dimensional object. It tells you exactly how much material is needed to cover or wrap a 3D shape — critical for manufacturing, packaging design, and construction.

---

## Formulas for Common 3D Shapes

### Rectangular Box (Cuboid): $l \times w \times h$
$$ SA = 2(lw + lh + wh) $$

### Cylinder (radius $r$, height $h$):
$$ SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh $$
*(Two circular caps + the lateral surface)*

### Sphere (radius $r$):
$$ SA = 4\pi r^2 $$

### Cone (radius $r$, slant height $s$):
$$ SA = \pi r^2 + \pi r s $$

### Pyramid (square base with side $a$, slant height $s$):
$$ SA = a^2 + 4 \times \frac{1}{2}as = a^2 + 2as $$

---

**Example (Cylinder):** A cylinder with radius 3 cm and height 10 cm:
$$ SA = 2\pi(3)^2 + 2\pi(3)(10) = 18\pi + 60\pi = 78\pi \approx 245.04 \text{ cm}^2 $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between surface area and volume?**
Surface area measures the 2D skin of a 3D object (square units). Volume measures the 3D space inside it (cubic units). A tank's volume tells you how much liquid it holds; its surface area tells you how much metal was needed to build it.

**What is a net (unfolded shape)?**
A net is a 2D pattern that folds into a 3D shape. The surface area equals the total area of the net. Calculating surface area is essentially finding the area of the unfolded flat version of the shape.
