## About the Triangle Calculator

Our **Triangle Calculator** solves for all sides, angles, area, and perimeter of any triangle. Whether it is a right triangle, equilateral, isosceles, or any scalene triangle, you only need a few known values to determine the rest.

---

## Area Formulas

**When base and height are known:**
$$ A = \frac{1}{2} \times b \times h $$

**Heron's Formula (when all three sides are known):**
$$ s = \frac{a + b + c}{2} \quad \text{(semi-perimeter)} $$
$$ A = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)} $$

**Using two sides and the included angle:**
$$ A = \frac{1}{2} ab \sin(C) $$

## The Law of Sines and Law of Cosines

These two laws let you solve any triangle from partial information:

**Law of Sines:**
$$ \frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} $$

**Law of Cosines:**
$$ c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \cos(C) $$

**Example (Law of Cosines):** Find side $c$ of a triangle with $a = 5$, $b = 7$, angle $C = 60°$:
$$ c^2 = 25 + 49 - 2(5)(7)\cos(60°) = 74 - 70(0.5) = 74 - 35 = 39 $$
$$ c = \sqrt{39} \approx 6.24 $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the interior angle rules for triangles?**
The three interior angles of any triangle always sum to exactly **180°**. This is true for all triangles regardless of size or shape.

**What is an oblique triangle?**
Any triangle that does NOT contain a 90° angle. Oblique triangles are solved using the Law of Sines or Law of Cosines rather than basic trigonometry.

**What makes a triangle impossible?**
A set of three lengths cannot form a triangle if the sum of any two sides is less than or equal to the third side. This is the Triangle Inequality: $a + b > c$.
