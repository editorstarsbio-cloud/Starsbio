## About the Volume Calculator

Our **Volume Calculator** helps you find the three-dimensional space inside any geometric shape. Volume calculations are needed in construction (how much concrete fills a cylinder), shipping (how much water a tank holds), chemistry (measuring liquid capacity), and cooking (recipe scaling).

---

## Formulas for Common Shapes

### Rectangular Box (Cuboid)
$$ V = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} \times \text{Height} $$

### Cylinder
$$ V = \pi r^2 h $$
*(where $r$ = radius, $h$ = height)*

### Cone
$$ V = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h $$

### Sphere
$$ V = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3 $$

### Pyramid
$$ V = \frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height} $$

---

**Example (Cylinder):** A cylindrical water tank with a 2-meter radius and 5-meter height:
$$ V = \pi \times 2^2 \times 5 = \pi \times 4 \times 5 = 62.83 \text{ m}^3 $$

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I convert cubic feet to gallons?**
1 cubic foot = 7.481 US gallons. Multiply your volume in cubic feet by 7.481.

**What is the volume of a hollow cylinder (like a pipe)?**
Calculate the volume of the outer cylinder minus the volume of the inner hole:
$$ V = \pi h (R^2 - r^2) $$
where $R$ is the outer radius and $r$ is the inner radius.
